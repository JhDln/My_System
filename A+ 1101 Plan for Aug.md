## HOW TO USE THIS CALENDAR

**Daily routine (30 min total):**

1.  **Open your Anki deck** (AnkiDroid on phone or Anki on laptop)
2.  **Review cards for 10 min** (do the cards marked "due," then new cards if time)
3.  **Open Professor Messer YouTube video** - play at 1.25x speed if needed
4.  **Watch for 12 minutes** - pause and take 1-2 quick notes if needed
5.  **Find a practice question** - use CompTIA objectives or free online practice tests
6.  **Log your progress** - screenshot or write down the numbers for your weekly check

**Where to find practice questions:**

-   CompTIA official study guides (if you have them)
-   Free: Professor Messer's website (messer.com) has free practice exams
-   Free: Total Seminars' A+ exam simulator (limited free version)
-   Paid: ExamCompass.com, Crucial Exams, or PrepLogic

----------

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

✅ **Do this:**

-   Stick to the 30min daily commitment (even if life is chaotic)
-   Watch Messer at 1.25-1.5x speed to save time
-   Review Anki cards BEFORE watching videos (priming your brain)
-   Log your progress every day (gives you momentum)
-   Do your weekly check every Sunday (keeps you accountable)

❌ **Don't do this:**

-   Skip Anki reviews thinking you'll "catch up later" (you won't)
-   Watch videos passively without taking notes
-   Do practice questions without understanding WHY you got it wrong
-   Cram all 30min into one weekend session (distributed learning works better)
